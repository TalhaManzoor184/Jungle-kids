# Jungle Kids — series bible

A daily 25–30 second vertical comedy reel. One everyday Pakistani situation (garmi, bijli chali gayi,
exam ki raat, shaadi ka khana, Eid, cricket match, Monday...) hits the jungle. Three friends suffer in
their own funny way, then the fourth one, **Chill Bhai**, shows up completely unbothered. The reel ends
with the question "Tum kaun ho? 1, 2, 3 ya 4?" so viewers comment a number.

Audience: general audience / family comedy (teens and adults who grew up with cartoons). Not made for kids.
Captions are in Roman Urdu, short and punchy. No dialogue is spoken; jokes land through action, sound
effects and on-screen captions.

Visual style: polished 3D animated family comedy (Pixar-like quality), bright tropical jungle,
soft cinematic sunlight, saturated colors, exaggerated cartoon expressions. Original characters only.

## The cast (always in this order)

1. **Sher** — `cast/sher.png`
   A big lazy lion with a huge fluffy orange-brown mane, golden fur, droopy tired eyes, thick dark
   eyebrows, pink tongue that often hangs out. Thinks he is the king but is always the most dramatic
   sufferer. Over-reacts to everything.

2. **Bhaalu** — `cast/bhaalu.png`
   A round chubby brown bear with a cream-colored muzzle, small round ears, sleepy happy face.
   Foodie and lazy: every problem is solved with food, sleep or the fridge.

3. **Hathi** — `cast/hathi.png`
   A cheerful baby elephant, light grey skin, big pink-lined floppy ears, rosy cheeks, always laughing
   with eyes squeezed shut. Optimist who turns every problem into a game with his trunk.

4. **Chill Bhai** — `cast/chill_bhai.png`
   A cool teal-green lizard with yellow spots, orange sunglasses, a turquoise Hawaiian shirt with pink
   and orange hibiscus flowers, a flower lei necklace, yellow shorts with a palm-tree print, and orange
   flip-flops. Long curled teal tail with yellow bands. Always confident, smug smile, totally unbothered.
   He is the twist at the end of every reel.

## Rules
- Each character keeps exactly the design in its reference image in every frame.
- One action per shot, easy to read in 5–6 seconds.
- No text, letters, logos or watermarks inside the generated images or videos (captions are added later).
- No real people, brands, politics or religion jokes. No violence beyond slapstick.
